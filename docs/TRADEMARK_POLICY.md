# TuwaiqOS Name and Trademark Policy

**Effective date:** August 9, 2026  
**Project:** TuwaiqOS  
**Repository:** <https://github.com/italamrii/TuwaiqOS>

## 1. Purpose

TuwaiqOS is an open-source operating-system project. This policy protects users and contributors from confusion about which software, releases, websites, services, and communities are officially associated with the project.

The source code is licensed separately under the repository's MIT License. That license permits broad use, modification, and distribution of the code, but it does not automatically grant permission to use the TuwaiqOS project name, official logo, or visual identity in a way that suggests endorsement or official status.

## 2. Protected Project Identity

For this policy, the **TuwaiqOS Marks** include:

- `TuwaiqOS`;
- `Tuwaiq OS`;
- `طويق أو إس` when used to identify this operating-system project;
- the official TuwaiqOS logo, icons, badges, and distinctive visual identity;
- confusingly similar names or branding used in connection with operating systems, software, technical services, or project communities.

This policy concerns the combined TuwaiqOS project identity. It does **not** claim exclusive ownership of the geographic or historical word `Tuwaiq` by itself or prevent fair use of that word in unrelated contexts.

## 3. Uses Allowed Without Prior Permission

You may use the TuwaiqOS name without prior written permission when the use is truthful, does not imply endorsement, and follows this policy, including:

- referring to or discussing the official TuwaiqOS project;
- linking to the official repository, website, documentation, or community;
- publishing accurate reviews, tutorials, research, compatibility reports, or news coverage;
- stating that software is compatible with, built for, or tested with TuwaiqOS;
- using screenshots of an unmodified official release for educational or editorial purposes;
- identifying a contribution that has been submitted to the official project;
- using the name in non-commercial community events or study groups, provided they are clearly described as independent and unofficial.

Whenever reasonable, include a statement such as:

> TuwaiqOS is an open-source project. This work is independent and is not endorsed by the official TuwaiqOS project.

## 4. Uses Requiring Written Permission

Prior written permission from the TuwaiqOS maintainers is required to:

- name a product, company, service, hosted platform, distribution, or commercial offering after TuwaiqOS;
- use the official logo or visual identity as the primary branding of another project;
- register a domain name, social-media account, application-store listing, organization, or community that may appear official;
- sell merchandise bearing the official TuwaiqOS logo or branding;
- advertise paid support, certification, training, consulting, or hosting as officially approved by TuwaiqOS;
- publish a modified build under the TuwaiqOS name or logo without clearly identifying the changes and obtaining approval;
- claim partnership, sponsorship, certification, endorsement, ownership, or official representation.

## 5. Forks and Modified Distributions

You may fork and modify the source code under the MIT License. However, a modified distribution must not be presented as an official TuwaiqOS release.

Unless written permission is granted, a publicly distributed modified build must:

1. use a distinct name and primary logo;
2. clearly state that it is an independent fork of TuwaiqOS;
3. identify material modifications;
4. avoid official version numbers, release signatures, or channels that could create confusion;
5. preserve copyright and license notices required by the MIT License.

You may truthfully write “based on TuwaiqOS” in a secondary descriptive manner.

## 6. Prohibited Uses

The TuwaiqOS Marks must not be used:

- to impersonate the project, its founder, maintainers, or contributors;
- to distribute malware, deceptive software, unauthorized telemetry, or compromised builds;
- to falsely imply official approval, security certification, government endorsement, or institutional adoption;
- in phishing, fraud, domain squatting, misleading fundraising, or deceptive recruitment;
- in a way that damages the project, misleads users about software origin, or hides significant modifications;
- in connection with unlawful, abusive, or malicious activity.

## 7. Official Releases and Integrity

Only releases, checksums, signatures, repositories, and download links published through official TuwaiqOS channels should be described as official.

Mirrors must clearly identify themselves as mirrors, preserve integrity information when available, and must not alter release files while presenting them as official.

## 8. Contributions

Submitting code, documentation, designs, tests, or other contributions does not transfer ownership of the TuwaiqOS project identity and does not grant authority to represent the project.

Contributors may accurately describe their own accepted contributions and participation.

## 9. Permission Requests and Reports

To request permission or report suspected misuse, contact the repository owner through the official GitHub repository:

<https://github.com/italamrii/TuwaiqOS>

Please include:

- your name and organization, if applicable;
- the proposed use;
- where and how the name or logo will appear;
- whether the use is commercial;
- relevant links, designs, screenshots, or distribution details.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the process described in `SECURITY.md`, not through a public issue.

## 10. Enforcement and Changes

The project maintainers may request correction, clarification, rebranding, or removal of uses that violate this policy or create a reasonable likelihood of confusion.

Permission may include conditions and may be withdrawn if those conditions are violated. This policy may be updated as the project and its legal protections develop. Material changes will be recorded in the repository history.
